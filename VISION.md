# VISION — What We Are Building

*This is the master file. It describes what the thing IS, not how it gets made.
Every new idea gets added here. Nothing gets deleted, only added or corrected.*

Last updated: September 2, 2026

---

## The One-Sentence Version

A place where you drop in a TikTok, and it turns into a clean, organized list of
places to go — so you never have to scrub back through videos again.

---

## The Problem We Are Solving

Right now, recommendations live trapped inside videos.

You see a TikTok about a great taco spot. You save it. Two weeks later you're in
that city, hungry, and you're scrolling your saved folder trying to remember
which video had the taco place. You watch it again. You pause it to read a name
on the screen. You open Maps in another app and type the name in. You do this
five more times for five more videos.

All of that information already exists. It's just locked in a format you can't
use when you actually need it — standing on a street corner deciding where to go.

**We are unlocking it.**

---

## What The Product Does

A person gives us a TikTok. We give them back an itinerary.

### How they give it to us — two ways

1. **Share the link.** They copy the TikTok link and paste it in (or share it
   straight to us from TikTok). This is the main way. It should feel like
   nothing — paste, done.

2. **Upload the video.** If they already downloaded the video to their phone,
   they can hand us the video file directly. Same result.

Both roads lead to the same place. The person should never have to think about
which one they're using.

### What they get back

Every recommendation from that video, pulled out and laid down as a list they
can actually act on. Not a transcript. Not a summary. A **plan**.

For each place, they should know:

- **What it is** — the name
- **Why it's here** — what the video said about it, in a sentence
- **What kind of place** — food, sight, activity, drink, shop
- **Where it is** — enough to find it

### The feeling we're after

Open the app. See the plan. Go.

No searching. No re-watching. No "let me check something real quick." The answer
is already sitting there, organized, waiting.

---

## The Core Promise

> **A video goes in. A day you can actually live goes out.**

Everything we build gets measured against that. If a feature doesn't move a
person closer to walking out the door with a plan, it isn't part of this.

---

## Who This Is For

Someone who saves travel and food content faster than they can ever use it.
They have 200 saved videos and have acted on maybe four. The gap between
"I saved that" and "I'm standing there eating it" is where this product lives.

---

## Where This Grows (Not Now — Later)

Written down so we don't forget, not because we're building it today.

- **More sources than TikTok.** Instagram, YouTube, a blog post, a screenshot,
  a friend's text message. Anything with a recommendation buried in it.
- **Many videos, one trip.** Five different TikToks about Tokyo collapse into a
  single Tokyo list, with duplicates merged.
- **A real map view.** See the whole list as pins, so you can tell what's
  walkable from what.
- **Order it by day.** Turn the list into Day 1, Day 2, Day 3 — grouped by what
  makes sense to do together.
- **Share the plan.** Send the finished itinerary to whoever you're traveling
  with.

---

## Rules We Hold Ourselves To

1. **Nothing invented.** If the video didn't say it, we don't put it on the list.
   A made-up restaurant is worse than no restaurant.
2. **Zero setup.** No forms, no tagging, no organizing work pushed onto the
   person. They paste, we do everything else.
3. **Fast enough to not walk away.** Paste it, and the plan shows up while
   they're still looking at the screen.
4. **Useful when standing up.** This gets used on a phone, outside, with one
   hand, possibly with bad signal. That's the real test.

---

## Open Questions

Things we haven't decided yet. Not blockers — just things to answer as we go.

- Does a person keep separate lists per trip or city, or one big running list?
- What happens when a video mentions a place too vaguely to pin down?
- Can a person edit the list afterward — add their own stops, cross things off?
