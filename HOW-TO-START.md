# How To Start It

## Every time you want to use it

Double-click **start.command**

A black window opens (that's normal — leave it alone) and the site opens in your
browser by itself.

## To stop it

Close that black window.

## If the browser doesn't open on its own

Go to your browser and type this in the address bar:

    127.0.0.1:8000

## What "local" means here

This runs on your computer only. Nobody else can see it. There's no account, no
bill, no internet company involved. When you close the window, it's off.

## Where your stuff lives

Everything you save goes in the **library** folder, one folder per post. You can
open it in Finder and look at the files directly — they're just normal photos,
videos, and sound files.
